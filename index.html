Text2Link

The self-contained hyperlink tool for Google Sheets

About the Add-on

Text2Link is a lightweight, secure productivity tool designed to convert text strings into functional, clickable hyperlinks directly inside your active spreadsheet. It is built entirely on Google Apps Script and operates with minimal permissions.

Security & Operational Model

  • No External Infrastructure: The add-on uses zero external databases, APIs, or third-party web servers. Your data stays entirely inside Google.
  • Scoped Execution: Text2Link explicitly utilizes the restricted @OnlyCurrentDoc scope. It is strictly prohibited from accessing, viewing, or modifying any other files in your Google Drive.
  • User Privacy First: Because the execution environment is completely isolated within your active document, no user data, text strings, or sheet metrics are ever collected, stored, or transmitted to outside networks.
